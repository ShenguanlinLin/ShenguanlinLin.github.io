Reading seminar · 2026–27

Seminar on Berkeley Lectures on p-adic Geometry

From classical deformation spaces to integral local Shimura varieties

Planned · tentative syllabus

We are planning a 17-session reading seminar based primarily on Peter Scholze and Jared Weinstein's Berkeley Lectures on p-adic Geometry (2020). Our aim is to work from foundational examples in adic and perfectoid geometry to diamonds, shtukas, Rapoport–Zink spaces, and local Shimura varieties.

Speakers: Xiaowei Li (odd-numbered sessions) and Shenguanlin Lin (even-numbered sessions). The order and content may be adjusted as the seminar progresses.
