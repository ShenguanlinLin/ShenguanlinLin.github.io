Research & mathematical writing

Shenguanlin Lin

I am a second-year Master’s student in the ALGANT programme at Université de Bordeaux (M2 AGTN), having completed my first year at the University of Regensburg. I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) in 2025.

My interests lie in arithmetic geometry and p-adic Hodge theory, particularly perfectoid spaces and Fargues–Fontaine curves.
